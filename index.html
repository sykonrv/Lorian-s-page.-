Bastard's Rebellion

View on Wattpad

Prince Daeron Targaryen was born to rule the Seven Kingdoms, yet in the eyes of his father, King Aegon IV, blood was never enough. The true blow fell not with a decree, but with a blade: Blackfyre, the legendary Valyrian steel sword of Aegon the Conqueror, bestowed upon Daemon Waters—a bastard born of passion, favored by a king who despised his own lawful heir.

What followed was not merely a single war, but a generational struggle for the Iron Throne. Dive into an expanded retelling of the Blackfyre Rebellions—an epic saga of dragons without wings, brotherhood turned to ashes, secret alliances, and a dynasty split down the middle.

"When the Black Dragon rises, will the Red Dragon burn?"

Dune Universe (Original Expansion)

Dune: Hunted Blood

In Development

Set against the treacherous backdrop of the desert planet Arrakis, Hunted Blood follows the imperiled line of House Talos, a minor noble house harboring a secret capable of upending the delicate balance of the Imperium. Hidden deep within their genetic heritage is an unauthorized Bene Gesserit mutation: a rare bloodline trait that grants its bearers complete immunity to Truthsayers and the ability to sever the prescient sight of Spacing Guild Navigators.

When an illicit alliance between Tleilaxu Face Dancers and an imperial inquisitor uncovers the anomaly, a ruthless campaign is launched to erase House Talos from the official imperial archives. Kaelen Talos, the surviving young heir, is thrust into a desperate flight across the deep desert alongside a hardened remnant of Fedaykin warriors. To survive the ultimate purge, Kaelen must master his dangerous legacy before those hunting his blood alter the fate of the known universe.
